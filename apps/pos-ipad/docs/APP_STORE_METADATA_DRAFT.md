## Description

`HB POS is the iPad checkout application for authorised Hot Bargain stores.`

`Store teams can scan or search products, build a cart, apply approved discounts, complete supported tenders, issue receipts, review local transaction history, process returns, manage held orders and recover interrupted payments. Device registration, store scope and cashier permissions are controlled by the organisation.`

`The application is designed for managed retail operations. It includes an encrypted local ledger, resilient offline cash workflows, auditable synchronisation and recovery controls for supported payment providers. Some functions require approved store hardware or an authorised backend configuration.`

`HB POS does not provide public account registration and is not intended for consumer use.`
